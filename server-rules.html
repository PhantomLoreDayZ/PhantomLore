🛡️ 1. Respect Other Players Treat all players with respect. Harassment, insults, discrimination or toxic behavior will not be tolerated. Avoid arguments in Global Chat. If you have a problem, contact the staff. ⚔️ 2. PVE Server This is a PvE server. Killing, attempting to kill or intentionally harming other players is strictly forbidden. Baiting infected, animals or bosses onto other players with the intention of killing them is not allowed. Blocking players from progressing through content is prohibited. 🏠 3. Base Building Raiding player bases is not allowed. Do not glitch into bases or exploit bugs to enter them. Do not build in ways that block roads, military areas, trader entrances or important game content. Staff may remove structures that negatively affect gameplay. Maximum group size 4 players. You are allowed to have 2 buildings, a small house and another of your choice - more information in the guides section on Discord. 🎯 4. Events & Loot Respect other players during events. First come, first served. Do not intentionally steal loot from players who arrived at the event before you. 🚗 5. Vehicles Stealing vehicles is strictly prohibited. Each player is responsible for keeping their vehicle securely locked and safe. We recommend using parking to store your vehicles. Public parking is available at many gas stations on the map, and you can also purchase private parking for your base. Vehicles left unused for 7 days will be automatically deleted by the system. The administration is not responsible for lost or stolen vehicles or their contents. 💬 6. Chat Rules Keep Global Chat respectful. No racism, hate speech, excessive toxicity or personal attacks. Advertising other servers is forbidden. English is recommended in Global Chat so everyone can understand. ⚙️ 7. Exploits & Cheats The following will result in immediate punishment: Hacks or cheats. Duplication of items. Abuse of bugs or unintended mechanics. Third-party software that provides an unfair advantage. If you discover a bug, report it to the staff instead of abusing it. 🤝 8. Fair Play Play fairly. Do not: Abuse server mechanics. Block access to traders or important locations. Intentionally ruin another player's experience. Exploit safe zones or event mechanics. 👮 9. Staff Decisions Staff decisions are final. Staff members may investigate suspicious situations. False reports or attempts to deceive staff may result in sanctions. The team can restructure the regulations at any time to adapt them to the server's needs. ⚠️ 10. Penalties Depending on the severity of the offense, penalties may include: Warning Temporary kick Temporary ban Permanent ban Repeated offenses will receive increasingly severe punishments. 🎫 11. Ticket Please remember to use the ticket system to contact the administration. Private messages may result in a penalty. Please use the appropriate communication channels. 💝 12. Donations Donations help fund the development and maintenance of Phantom Lore. No donations offer any in-game advantages. Every donation is voluntary and helps cover server hosting, development, licensing, and community maintenance costs. Sponsors are subject to the same rules as all other players. 💳 13. Monetization Packages Purchasing a Phantom Lore monetization package does not grant immunity from the server rules. All players are treated equally regardless of any purchase. Cosmetic purchases do not exempt players from warnings, kicks, temporary bans or permanent bans. Staff decisions always take precedence over any purchased service. 🌑 Enjoy Your Adventure Phantom Lore is built around cooperation, exploration and survival. Help other survivors, enjoy the world, take part in events and become part of the growing Phantom Lore community.
